## What Is Permit Hub?

Permit Hub is an Android permission management and app risk assessment tool designed to help users understand which permissions their installed apps request and identify applications that may warrant additional attention.

The app evaluates installed applications using factors such as:

- Requested permissions
- Permission usage patterns
- Publicly known security indicators
- Permission sensitivity levels

Based on this analysis, applications are categorized into different risk levels, making it easier for users to prioritize which apps they should review first.

This approach helps users quickly identify apps that may have excessive permissions or access to sensitive device capabilities.

## Key Features of Permit Hub

### 1. App Risk Assessment

One of the most powerful features of Permit Hub is its risk assessment system.

Installed applications are categorized into four levels:

- High Risk
- Medium Risk
- Low Risk
- No Risk

These classifications help users quickly determine which applications may deserve closer review.

#### High Risk Apps

High Risk apps may request excessive or sensitive permissions or demonstrate behavior patterns that warrant additional attention.

When an app receives a High Risk classification, users should review its permissions and determine whether the app's access level is appropriate for its intended functionality.

#### Medium Risk Apps

Medium Risk applications may request more permissions than appear necessary.

This classification does not automatically indicate that an app is unsafe, but it may justify a closer inspection.

#### Low Risk Apps

Low Risk apps generally have minor permission concerns according to Permit Hub's evaluation criteria.

#### No Risk Apps

Apps classified as No Risk typically request minimal permissions and have no known permission-related concerns based on the app's assessment.

> Important: Risk classifications are intended as guidance rather than definitive proof that an application is safe or unsafe.

## Permit Hub and Android Accessibility Service

Permit Hub utilizes Android's AccessibilityService API to assist users in understanding and navigating permission-related information.

Because Accessibility Services provide powerful device capabilities, transparency regarding their usage is important.

According to Permit Hub's functionality, Accessibility Services are used specifically to:

- Guide users to permission settings
- Read permission-related information displayed on screen
- Present information in a simpler format
- Help identify permission usage patterns

The service is intended to improve visibility and navigation—not to automatically control the device.

## Does Permit Hub Automatically Change Permissions?

No.

Permit Hub does not automatically grant or revoke permissions.

All permission-related decisions remain under the user's control.

The app is designed to provide information and guidance while allowing users to decide how they wish to manage app permissions.

## What Permit Hub Does Not Do

Transparency is an important aspect of privacy-focused applications.

According to the developer's disclosures, Permit Hub does not:

- Automatically change system settings
- Grant permissions without user action
- Revoke permissions without user action
- Prevent app uninstallation
- Take control of the device
- Bypass Android privacy protections
- Record audio
- Record screen content
- Collect personal data through Accessibility Services for unrelated purposes

Accessibility Services are optional and activated only after user consent.
