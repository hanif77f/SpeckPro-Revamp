## WiFi Signal Strength Analyzer

One of the most important factors affecting wireless performance is WiFi signal strength.

A device may show that it is connected to WiFi, but that does not necessarily mean the signal is strong enough for reliable performance.

WiFiHorizon lets users monitor WiFi signal strength using dBm measurements and percentage indicators.

Real-time information and graphs can make it easier to understand how signal quality changes as you move around your home or office.

This can help answer questions such as:

- Is my router signal strong enough?
- Which room has the weakest WiFi?
- How much does signal strength change between rooms?
- Is moving closer to the router improving the connection?
- Where should I consider improving WiFi coverage?

Understanding these patterns can be particularly useful when troubleshooting slow connections, buffering, dropped connections, or unstable video calls.

## Find WiFi Dead Zones

WiFi dead zones are areas where wireless coverage becomes weak or unreliable.

They can occur because of:

- Distance from the router
- Walls and floors
- Furniture
- Building materials
- Wireless interference
- Router placement
- Network configuration

WiFiHorizon includes a walk mode that helps users examine WiFi signal conditions as they move around a space.

This makes it possible to identify areas where signal strength drops significantly.

For example, you could walk from your router to a bedroom, office, kitchen, basement, or another area and observe how the WiFi signal changes.

This can help you identify potential WiFi dead zones and determine where additional coverage improvements may be useful.
